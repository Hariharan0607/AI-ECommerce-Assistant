\## Objective

\## Required Features



\### 1. Order Management

\- Display e-commerce orders.

\- Process order information.

\- Track order status.

\- Display customer, product and order amount.



\### 2. Fraud Detection

\- Analyze suspicious orders.

\- Detect high-value transactions.

\- Detect pending orders.

\- Calculate fraud risk score.

\- Classify risk as Low, Medium or High.



\### 3. Customer Analysis

\- Analyze customer purchase history.

\- Calculate total orders.

\- Calculate customer spending.

\- Segment customers into New, Returning, Loyal or VIP.

\- Provide recommendations.



\### 4. Product and Revenue Analysis

\- Calculate total revenue.

\- Calculate total orders.

\- Calculate average order value.

\- Track delivered and pending orders.

\- Display business analytics.



\### 5. Report Generation

\- Generate an AI-based business report.

\- Summarize order and revenue performance.

\- Provide business recommendations.



\### 6. AI Assistant

\- Provide an interactive assistant interface.

\- Answer questions about orders.

\- Provide fraud information.

\- Explain customer analysis.

\- Provide revenue information.

\- Explain generated reports.



\## Multi-Agent Architecture



Create the following agents:



1\. Order Agent

2\. Fraud Detection Agent

3\. Customer Analysis Agent

4\. Analysis Agent

5\. Report Agent

6\. Orchestrator Agent



\## Agent Responsibilities



\### Order Agent

Processes and tracks e-commerce orders.



\### Fraud Detection Agent

Evaluates transaction risk and identifies suspicious orders.



\### Customer Analysis Agent

Analyzes customer behavior and creates customer segments.



\### Analysis Agent

Analyzes overall e-commerce business performance.



\### Report Agent

Generates business reports and recommendations.



\### Orchestrator Agent

Coordinates all specialized agents and combines their results into a unified response.
