\# AI E-Commerce Assistant - Features



\## 1. Order Management

\- Create and process e-commerce orders.

\- Track order status.

\- Display customer, product and transaction information.

\- Connect order processing with the Order Agent.



\## 2. Fraud Detection

\- Analyze high-value transactions.

\- Detect suspicious pending orders.

\- Calculate a fraud risk score.

\- Classify orders as Low, Medium or High risk.



\## 3. Customer Analysis

\- Analyze customer order history.

\- Calculate customer spending.

\- Segment customers into New, Returning, Loyal and VIP categories.

\- Generate customer recommendations.



\## 4. Business Analytics

\- Calculate total revenue.

\- Calculate total order count.

\- Calculate average order value.

\- Track delivered and pending orders.



\## 5. AI Report Generation

\- Generate business reports.

\- Summarize revenue and order performance.

\- Provide operational recommendations.

\- Combine results from multiple AI agents.



\## 6. AI Assistant

\- Answer questions about orders.

\- Provide fraud information.

\- Explain customer analytics.

\- Provide revenue information.

\- Explain generated reports.



\## 7. Multi-Agent Orchestration

\- Coordinate multiple specialized agents.

\- Pass information between agents.

\- Combine agent outputs into a unified workflow.

\- Support scalable enterprise AI deployment.
